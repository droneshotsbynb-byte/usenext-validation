Plain-language summary · reviewed 30 August 2026

Privacy without fine-print theatre.

The Android beta keeps your manually entered food list on your phone. The validation site has no account, form backend, payment system or public APK.

Android debug build

UseNext stores item names, locations, quantities, optional use-by dates and onboarding state locally in Android app preferences on your phone. It has no account, backend, Internet permission, runtime permissions, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. It does not sync, share or export your list.

Removing an item deletes it from the app list. Uninstalling the app removes its app data under normal Android behaviour. Do not enter sensitive personal information. UseNext is not food-safety advice and does not determine whether food is safe.

Validation website

This static GitHub Pages site has no visitor analytics at present. It has no account, form backend, payment system or public APK, and our site code sets no cookies.

Beta requests and price answers use email links. Nothing is submitted to this website: your chosen email app sends your message to droneshotsbynb@gmail.com, where it is handled by your email provider and Gmail. Do not include sensitive food, health or device identifiers.

Questions or deletion

Email droneshotsbynb@gmail.com to ask a privacy question or request deletion of a beta or price-response email.
